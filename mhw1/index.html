The Foundation

A legacy of quiet generosity

The Marvin H. Weiner Foundation provides funding for worthy causes in the areas of healthcare, education, science, social welfare, and the environment. Established and endowed by Marvin H. Weiner, the Foundation carries forward the belief that guided his life: that success is something to be shared.
