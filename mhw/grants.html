Grants

The Foundation supports organizations doing direct, meaningful work for people in need — with particular attention to those least able to help themselves.

Where We Give

01

Healthcare

Supporting medical research and organizations that bring care to people who could not otherwise afford it — with an emphasis on direct patient impact.

02

Education

Funding programs that open educational doors for students of every age and circumstance, from early learning through higher education.

03

Science

Backing scientific inquiry and innovation in the spirit of the Foundation's founder — an inventor who believed better solutions could always be built.

04

Helping organizations that meet essential human needs — food, shelter, safety, and dignity — for individuals and families facing hardship.

The Environment

Contributing to the protection and stewardship of the natural world for the generations to come.

Eligibility

The Foundation reviews a limited number of proposals each year. Please confirm that your organization meets the following criteria before submitting.

  • Tax-exempt status. Your organization is recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) public charity in good standing.
  • Area of focus. Your work falls within one of the Foundation's five areas: healthcare, education, science, social welfare, or the environment.
  • Direct impact. Funding will support programs and services with direct, demonstrable benefit to the people you serve.
  • Individuals. The Foundation does not make grants to individuals.
  • Reporting. Grant recipients agree to report on how funds were used, using the Foundation's reporting form.

If you meet the above requirements and would like to submit a grant request, please submit a formal proposal to info@mhweiner.org.
