Colour Palette Options

You asked for one of two directions — orange with greens or orange with blues — and there is a third worth seeing: orange leading. Use the switch to move between them. The ten palettes stay the same; what changes is the colour that runs the site. The brown is gone from all of them. The first switch is the Social Welfare orange: logo orange is #FF8B00 exactly, with its icon in black — the logo’s own pairing — and as first sent is the version you already have, where that orange was darkened so a cream icon would read on it.

How to choose

Tell me a direction and a name — for example “blue, Clay” — and I will build the site around it. Mixing is fine too: take the greens from one and the blue from another.

Nothing else on the site moves until this is settled, so a quick reply keeps us on track for the board meeting.
